## Track 4 — Phase 2 Foundations ✅

### 4.1 Surrogate AI Operators (Synthetic-Only)
* **Artifacts & Corpus:** `phase2/surrogate/*` + `data/synthetic_corpus/` (450 runs: 400 train / 50 eval @ $48^3$, deterministic).
* **Physics Modeling:** Hagen–Poiseuille + Young–Tsai stenosis + Murray split + TVD transport (qualitative ground truth, not full Navier-Stokes).
* **Models Trained:**
  * **GINO (1.41M params, FNO trunk):** Loss 1.65 → 0.51 (no NaNs; conditioning/collapse root-cause fix documented). Held-out relative $L_2$ error ($u/p$): 0.29 / 0.28; $\Delta p$ error: 24.5%. Single-thread CPU latency: 311.5 ms.
  * **Transolver (109k params, physics attention):** Loss 1.90 → 1.28. Held-out relative $L_2$ error ($u/p$): 0.82 / 0.81; $\Delta p$ error: 64% (capacity-limited). Single-thread CPU latency: 77.8 ms.
* **Notes & Flags:** Multi-thread execution degraded performance due to oversubscription (documented). Checkpoints preserved. Subsampling to grid-24 and 5/17 CPU epochs constrained final accuracy. $\Delta p$ corpus span (22–915 Pa) covers only the lower segment of the $10^2$–$10^4\text{ Pa}$ brief range (scale analysis demonstrates $10^4\text{ Pa}$ is unreachable within these domain dimensions).

### 4.2 0D/1D Reduced Order Models (ROM)
* **Artifacts:** `phase2/rom/*` using `svZeroDSolver` (`pysvzerod==2.0` compiled from source; bypasses openBF/Julia dependencies).
* **Domain:** Executed across 3 real coronary trees (21, 16, and 35 branches; centerlines spanning 659–1,071 mm).
* **Validation & Performance:**
  * Solver runtime: 1.26–1.58 s.
  * Mass balance: 225 mL/min exact, calibrated at 90 mmHg.
  * Labeled reference cross-check: $\le 0.07\%$ flow discrepancy, 0.6 ms offset.
  * Branch transit times resolved in milliseconds (case 601: 31–4,291 ms; arrival curves with 203–480 points + generated PNG plots).

### 4.3 Decoupled Transport
* **Artifacts:** `phase2/transport/*` evaluated across 27 injection configurations ($3 \times 3 \times 3$).
* **Speedup:** Mean **4.28×** ($2.90\text{–}5.28\times$ at $48^3$; $7.2\text{–}13.6\times$ at $64^3$). Coupled runtime: 1,684 s vs. Decoupled runtime: 447 s.
* **Accuracy & Linearity Finding:** Frozen-velocity outlet error is 27% median / 51% max. Under Stokes linearity, per-injection velocity is an exact scalar rescale:
  $$u(Q) = \left(\frac{Q}{Q_b}\right) u_b$$
  *(Verified numerical residual: $3 \times 10^{-15}$)*. This recovers coupled fidelity at decoupled operational cost.
* **Physics Integrity:** Poiseuille divergence 3–6%, numerical residuals $10^{-12}$, mass conservation $5 \times 10^{-6}$.
* **Optimization:** OpenBLAS thread spin-up latency reduced from 55 ms to 20 µs/op.

### 4.4 GPU Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) Evaluation
* **Artifact:** `docs/lbm_solver_evaluation.md` (339 lines, 29 comparative evaluation axes).
* **Ranked Recommendations:**
  1. **OpenLB 1.9.x (Primary):** Usable as a service under GPLv2; supports MRT/BGK, Zou–He/FD/convective boundary conditions, native one-way NS→ADE scalar coupling, runtime checkpoints, and CLI parameterization without recompilation (satisfies R4).
  2. **FluidX3D (Runner-Up):** Peak computational efficiency, but licensing strictly bars commercial and military use; lacks a dedicated scalar-only re-solver. Requires explicit commercial re-licensing and legal review.
  3. **HARVEY (Watch Item):** Duke proprietary licensing constraints.
* **Deployment Plan:** Cloud roadmap stages W0–W5 configured for measured geometry scale and R4 gate ($\le 2\text{ min}$, $\le 30\%$ rerun overhead) with decision gates G1–G4.
