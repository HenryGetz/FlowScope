Full cardiac fluid-structure interaction (FSI) is governed by a severe added-mass instability due to the matched physical density of blood and soft myocardial tissue ($\rho_s/\rho_f \approx 1.0$), historically demanding $10^4$ to $10^6$ CPU hours per cardiac cycle. Achieving clinically viable, near-real-time execution requires navigating a three-tier computational hierarchy: high-fidelity open-source multiphysics solvers (hours to days), GPU-accelerated matrix-free engines (hours down to minutes), and scientific machine learning (SciML) neural operator surrogates (sub-second / milliseconds).

## Production Open-Source Frameworks & Codebases

* **IBAMR (Immersed Boundary AMR):** Implements the landmark four-chamber, four-valve human heart model developed by Davey, Puelz, Rossi, Peskin, and Griffith. The fluid equations are solved on adaptive Cartesian grids, while the cardiac anatomy is discretized via Immersed Finite Elements (IFED) calibrated directly to human tissue tensile tests. Valvular leaflets coapt naturally via Eulerian-Lagrangian integral transforms, bypassing explicit contact detection and mesh regeneration.


* **lifeX (iHEART Project):** A modular C++17 finite element library built on deal.II and PETSc by MOX at Politecnico di Milano. It pairs an Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian (ALE) description for chamber walls with the Resistive Immersed Implicit Surface (RIIS) method for valve dynamics. To bypass expensive monolithic block iterations while avoiding added-mass divergence, lifeX uses explicit Robin-Neumann partitioned FSI schemes scaling across thousands of cores.


* **svMultiPhysics / SimVascular:** An open-source C++17 multi-physics platform connecting clinical DICOM workflows directly to monolithic block Newton-Krylov ALE solvers. It supports anisotropic constitutive models (Holzapfel-Gasser-Ogden, Guccione) alongside 0D lumped-parameter Windkessel networks for peripheral vascular impedance.


* **FEBioMFEMiFSI & Ambit:** FEBioMFEMiFSI couples FEBio’s hyperelastic leaflet contact coaptation mechanics with MFEM’s high-order, GPU-scalable fluid elements via Fictitious Domain Distributed Lagrange Multipliers. Ambit (FEniCSx backend) introduces Fluid-Reduced-Solid Interaction (FrSI), projecting wall elastodynamics onto compliant boundary subspaces to drastically cut computational overhead.



## GPU Acceleration: Reducing Days to Minutes

* **Matrix-Free Nodal Immersed Boundary (Ma, Cai, Wang, Gao):** Bypasses sparse matrix assembly bottlenecks by evaluating hyperelastic internal forces directly at structural nodes without intermediate finite element projections. The fluid domain is solved with Chorin's projection using matrix-free Geometric Multigrid (GMG), storing shape functions in GPU constant memory and accelerating semi-Lagrangian advection via hardware texture filtering. This delivers a 50× to 100× speedup over 20-core parallel CPU FEM solvers, completing full cardiac cycles in 2 to 3 hours on a single workstation GPU.


* **IB-Flows (Scarpolini, Viola, Verzicco):** A multi-GPU CUDA Fortran solver combining Moving Least Squares and sharp-interface immersed boundaries. It integrates NVIDIA's `cuDecomp` library to execute pencil-distributed memory transposes for 3D FFT pressure Poisson solves across GPU clusters.


* **HARVEY (Amanda Randles Lab):** Replaces continuum Navier-Stokes solvers with the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) on DOE leadership supercomputers. Its local streaming-collision kernels bypass global linear system inversions, scaling across Frontier and Aurora to resolve macroscopic flows and cellular mechanics simultaneously.



## The Bleeding Edge: Real-Time Surrogates (<100 ms)

* **Latent Neural ODEs (LNODEs):** Developed by Regazzoni, Salvador, Dedè, and Quarteroni, LNODEs compress high-dimensional 3D electromechanical continuum mechanics into compact latent dynamical systems ($\dot{z} = \text{NN}(z, P; \mu)$) (Salvador et al., 2024). Bidirectionally coupled to 0D lumped-parameter vascular circuits, a compact 3-layer neural network evaluates full cardiac cycles in under 100 ms (>3000× speedup) on consumer laptop CPUs (Salvador et al., 2024).


* **Heterogeneous Graph Attention (HGATSolver):** Formulates coupled multiphysics as a heterogeneous graph with dedicated message-passing layers for fluid, solid, and interface nodes. A Physics-Conditioned Gating Mechanism (PCGM) prevents rollout drift, while an Inter-domain Gradient-Balancing Loss stabilizes training to achieve 10–50 ms inference.


* **Physics-Informed DeepONets & Hybrid FNOs:** PI-DeepONets process parametric hemodynamics in 0.5–5 ms by constraining loss functions with Navier-Stokes and hyperelastic residuals. Hybrid Fourier Neural Operators (FNOs) infer fluid velocity and pressure fields in 1–10 ms, passing surface tractions directly to boundary structural solvers.


* **Universal Ventricular Coordinates (UVCs) & Neural Fields:** Normalizes diverse patient ventricular geometries into regularized apicobasal, transmural, and rotational axes. Conditioned on DeepSDF latent shapes, neural fields predict 3D continuous displacement and stress distributions on unseen patient anatomies without retraining.
